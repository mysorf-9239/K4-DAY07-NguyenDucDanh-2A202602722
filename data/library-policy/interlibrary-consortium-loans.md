# Interlibrary and Consortium Loans

## Books & Media

Check HoyaSearch. Georgetown books located on HoyaSearch as "Available," allow patrons to look for the item on site.

If the item is not available at Georgetown, but is available at a consortium library, select Consortium Request.

If the item is not available at Georgetown or through the WRLC, then an Interlibrary Loan (ILL) may be available.

If the material you need is not available at Georgetown or Consortium Libraries, place a request through Interlibrary
Loan. After you place the request, ILL staff will attempt to find an available copy of the item from another library. If
the item you require is unavailable, ILL staff will let you know.

The source page provides ILL request options for:

- Georgetown University Hilltop and Capitol Faculty, Students and Staff
- Georgetown University Medical Center patrons (Dahlgren Memorial Library)
- Georgetown University Law Center patrons

## Articles

Check the journal title in Journal Finder or HoyaSearch first. If the desired journal volume and issue is not available,
go to step 2. If Lauinger, Woodstock, or Blommer Science Libraries have the journal but full text is available in hard
copy only, please go to step 2 and request the article through Interlibrary Loan (ILL).

Request the article through Interlibrary Loan (ILL).

The source page provides ILL request options for:

- Georgetown University Hilltop and Capitol Faculty, Students and Staff
- Georgetown University Medical Center patrons (Dahlgren Memorial Library)
- Georgetown University Law Center patrons

## Items from Other Locations

HoyaSearch items designated "Available at Lauinger Library Off-Campus Storage" or "Consortium Libraries" generally take
1-3 business days to arrive.

HoyaSearch items designated "Interlibrary Loan Request" may be shipped from around the world or other parts of the
country. Average delivery times for Interlibrary Loans are 7-14 business days.

## Recalls & Holds

### Hold Shelf Items

Notifications are sent for every item once the item has been placed on the hold shelf and is ready to be picked up.

### Recall Notices

Items recalled to be placed on Course Reserves must be returned in seven days, and take priority over items available to
an individual loan. Items that have been recalled for Course Reserves will be made available to students that need
access to materials. Course Reserves are especially useful for heavy or expensive textbooks, but may apply to any items
needed for a course.

Recall notices are automatically generated and e-mailed. You should regularly check your HoyaSearch Account on the
Library's website to check for recalls on your items in case the recall notification e-mail ends up in a spam folder.

Materials are subject to recall at ANY TIME. If you plan to be out of town for an extended period of time, please return
borrowed items or arrange with someone to receive notices and return recalled material for you. Main Campus libraries
are open during intersessions, so recalled items may be due any time the libraries are open.

### Recall Fines

Recall fines apply to all borrowers and are $5 per item per day. There is no maximum limit on recall fines.
